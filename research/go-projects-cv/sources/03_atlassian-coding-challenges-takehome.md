## Relevance
- Bears on hypotheses: H1 (engineering hygiene signals over showmanship), H3 (what evaluation of a submitted project actually looks like), H4 (level-independent criteria)
- This is the hiring side telling you exactly what it reads in a project: tests present, actually runs, requirements followed, sane tech choices, defensible design decisions — a checklist of engineering-hygiene signals, not domain cleverness (H1).
- The explicit warning against "odd or shiny things in order to impress us" plus the Atlassian bullet about candidates whose submissions don't work shows what annoys reviewers: over-tooling and unverifiable claims (H1, opposition evidence).
- "The finished product they send us" is read end-to-end — i.e. when a reviewer does engage with a project artifact, they run it and audit it against stated claims, so a CV project must be runnable and honestly described (H3).
