## Relevance
- Bears on hypotheses: H3 refutation via funnel arithmetic — if ATS filters remove ~75% of cold resumes and referrals produce 30-50% of hires (40-50% in tech), then the marginal impressiveness of a portfolio project on a cold CV is small relative to who vouches for you; H1 stress-test (projects are one input inside a funnel where referral status dominates stage-by-stage: ~25% of cold applicants even get a human resume read vs ~95% of referred).
- 3-line summary: A compendium of LinkedIn/Jobvite/SHRM/CareerXroads benchmarks shows hiring is referral-dominated: 5-7% of applicants but 30-50% of hires, 4x conversion, 29 vs 55 days to hire, and tech firms drawing 40-50% of hires from referrals. Combined with ATS-filter statistics, it implies the cold-application route — where a Go portfolio is supposed to differentiate — is where most candidates are screened out before any repo is seen. Caveat: vendor page aggregating secondary benchmarks, not primary data, though directionally consistent with academic referral literature.
